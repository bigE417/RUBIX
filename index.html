Cube notes

Why Rubik's Cube is still brilliant

A 3x3 cube has more than 43 quintillion reachable positions, but every valid scramble is handled by RUBIX with solutions under 25 moves. This app focuses on the useful version: show the state clearly, compute a solid solution, then let you step through it.

FAQ

Can I enter a real cube?

Yes. Turn Paint on, choose a color, then click stickers on the cube.

Why do controls lock after Solve?

It keeps the solved path tied to the exact cube state. Reset returns to editing.

What moves are supported?

Standard face turns: U, R, F, D, L, B, plus prime and double turns.
